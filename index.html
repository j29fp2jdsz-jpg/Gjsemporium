About GJ's Emporium

Old, unloved and tired furniture—given a new story.

Garry restores, refurbishes and reworks furniture from his base in Treharris. Some pieces begin with an existing item; others are designed around a customer's idea and measurements.

The result is useful furniture with character: carefully finished, completely individual and saved from being forgotten.
