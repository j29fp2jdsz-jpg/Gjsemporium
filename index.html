About GJ's Emporium · Treharris

Old, unloved and tired furniture—given a new story.

Garry restores, refurbishes and reworks furniture from his base in Treharris, South Wales. Some projects begin with an existing item that needs repairing or refinishing; others are designed around a customer's idea and measurements.

The result is useful furniture with character: carefully finished, completely individual and saved from being forgotten. If you have a piece that needs restoration or an idea for something bespoke, get in touch to discuss what is possible.

Furniture restoration FAQs

Restoration & bespoke furniture in South Wales

What furniture can GJ's Emporium restore?

Garry works with a wide range of freestanding furniture including drawers, sideboards, cabinets, tables and unusual one-off pieces. Send photos of the item to discuss whether restoration, repair or refinishing is suitable.

Do you make bespoke furniture?

Yes. Bespoke commissions can be designed around your available space, preferred colours and intended use. Past work includes bars, display furniture, tables and coffin-based furniture.

Where is GJ's Emporium based?

GJ's Emporium is based in Treharris and serves customers across South Wales. Contact Garry to discuss the piece, collection or delivery requirements for your location.
